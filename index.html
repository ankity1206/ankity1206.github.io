Research Project

DRDO Project

Funded cryptanalysis project on lattice-based post-quantum cryptography.

DRDO Funded Post-Quantum Cryptography NIST PQC Standards

Analysis of NIST's Lattice-based PQC Digital Signatures

Under the guidance of Prof. Sartaj Ul Hasan and Prof. Sumit Kumar Pandey.

Investigated mathematical foundations, security bounds, and algorithmic performance of candidate lattice-based digital signature schemes submitted to the NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) standardization process. The project evaluated signature verification efficiency, key generation mechanics, and underlying hard lattice problems (such as Module-LWE and Shortest Vector Problem variants) against classical and quantum reduction strategies.

Lattice Cryptanalysis Module-LWE / SIS Digital Signatures Shortest Vector Problem (SVP) Defence R&D
